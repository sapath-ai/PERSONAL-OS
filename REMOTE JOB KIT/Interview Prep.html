Frameworks
43
STARSituation, Task, Action, Result — for behavioral questions
44
CARChallenge, Action, Result — shorter version of STAR
45
WHAT-SO WHAT-NOW WHAT
What happened, what it meant, what's next — great for failures and lessons
46
Pyramid PrincipleLead with the answer, then support with reasoning — great for case-style questions
47
RICE / ICEReach × Impact × Confidence ÷ Effort — for prioritization questions
