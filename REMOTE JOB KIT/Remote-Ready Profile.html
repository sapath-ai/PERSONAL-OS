Build a Remote-Ready Profile
2
Before applying anywhere, build the profile remote employers actually look at. Work top-to-bottom — each section is a checklist.
3
4
Remote employers can't see you walk in. They can't read body language across a desk. They can ONLY see what you've put online — your resume, LinkedIn, GitHub, portfolio, writing, and how you communicate in writing.
5
That's why a 'remote-ready profile' is more than a resume. It's the complete digital trail that signals: this person can work without supervision, communicates clearly, and produces visible work.
6
Work through every section. The strongest remote candidates don't apply harder — they SHOW UP differently before they ever apply.

The 80/20 of getting hired remotely:
139
1. A strong LinkedIn + portfolio out-perform a perfect resume.
140
2. One referral beats 50 cold applications.
141
3. Public work (GitHub, blog posts, Looms, demos) compounds. Build it once, it works for years.
142
