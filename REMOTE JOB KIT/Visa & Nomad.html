Tax considerations (general)
45
Tax residency is generally triggered by spending 183+ days in a country in a tax year — but rules vary widely. Some countries use 'center of vital interests' (family, primary home) rather than days.
46
Working remotely in a country may create permanent establishment (PE) risk for your employer, even on a tourist visa. Some employers won't allow it without local tax registration.
47
US citizens are taxed on worldwide income regardless of where they live. The Foreign Earned Income Exclusion (FEIE) allows ~USD 126k exclusion (2025 figure) if you meet physical presence or bona fide residence tests.
48
Tax treaties between countries can prevent double taxation — check if a treaty exists between your home and host country.
49
Health insurance: most digital nomad visas require proof of international health coverage. SafetyWing, IMG Global, Cigna Global are common.
50
Always consult a cross-border tax professional before making moves with significant tax implications. Mistakes are expensive.
